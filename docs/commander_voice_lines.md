# Vael'Thor Commander – Voice Lines (Recording Script)

Voice direction: cold, measured, absolute. The Nexus does not panic and does not ask.
It states facts and issues decrees. Speak slowly, low register, slight reverb in post
(the voice of the Empire speaking through the Nexus). Keep each take under ~3 seconds.

Suggested file name in brackets. Record every variant; the game picks one at random.
